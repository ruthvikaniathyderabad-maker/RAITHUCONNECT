# Produce categories, units and demo prices

**Purpose:** Believable UI seed data for the RAITHUCONNECT prototype. These are illustrative demo listing prices, not live offers and not verified quotes from any individual farmer.

## Reference source
India's Department of Consumer Affairs Price Monitoring System reports retail and wholesale prices daily across market centres: https://fcainfoweb.nic.in/

The Exa research result showed the system's all-India average retail prices as of 27 October 2026:
- Potato: ₹22.69/kg
- Onion: ₹53.32/kg
- Tomato: ₹39.62/kg
- Banana: ₹49.78/kg
- Ginger: ₹33.48/kg
- Garlic: ₹45.32/kg

The source is an all-India retail reference for the stated date; it is not an Andhra Pradesh/Telangana mandi quote and is not a guaranteed farm-gate price. Quality, variety, season, transport, and market centre affect the price.
